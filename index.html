01

Profile

01 / 07

I come to transport planning from electrical and electronic engineering. After an Erasmus Mundus master’s in Sustainable Transport and Electrical Power Systems, I have spent nearly six years in doctoral research on transport decarbonisation at the University of Oviedo.

My work covers urban and interurban traffic studies, route and network assessment, transport modelling and scenario analysis. It brings together road-network, traffic-signal, elevation, GPS/OBD and geospatial data, and ends in clear technical reports for supervisors and multidisciplinary collaborators. Before that, I taught electrical and electronic engineering and led a university department in Bangladesh.

Outputs benchmarked against peer-reviewed studies and established transport and emissions models, with modelled fuel consumption checked against real-driving Torque OBD data.

  1. Conducted urban and interurban mobility studies using route geometry, road classification, speed limits, traffic signals, elevation and operating conditions.
  2. Applied PTV Visum, PTV Vissim and Aimsun alongside MATLAB and Python to develop and evaluate transport, traffic and vehicle-performance scenarios.
  3. Collected, cleaned and integrated multi-source GIS and mobility data from OpenStreetMap, routing and mapping APIs, GPS/OBD logs and time series.
  4. Developed comparative models and KPIs for conventional, hybrid and electric vehicles, linking route conditions and driver profiles with travel time, energy, emissions and efficiency.
  5. Designed scenario and sensitivity analyses on traffic signals, speed profiles, road conditions, driver behaviour and modelling assumptions.
  6. Translated methods and results into visualisations, technical reports, presentations and evidence-based recommendations.
